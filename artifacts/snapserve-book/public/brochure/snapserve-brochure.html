Overview
What is SnapServe?
SnapServe is an AI voice agent orchestration platform. It sits between your telephony provider and AI models — handling everything from speech recognition to response generation to text-to-speech — so your agents can have natural, intelligent conversations over any phone call.
🎙️
Automatic Speech Recognition
Real-time transcription of caller speech using best-in-class ASR providers. Supports Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, English, and more.
🧠
LLM Conversation Engine
Your agent's intelligence — powered by OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Mistral or Cohere. Custom system prompts, tools, and personas per agent.
🔊
Text-to-Speech
Natural-sounding voice output via ElevenLabs, OpenAI, AWS Polly, Azure, Cartesia, or Deepgram. Choose the voice that fits your brand.
📞
Telephony Integration
Works with Twilio, Plivo, VoBiz, and Vonage. Handles inbound calls, outbound campaigns, warm transfers, and IVR flows.
🧬
Caller Memory
Remembers facts, preferences, and history across every call. Your agent greets returning callers by name and never asks the same question twice.
📅
Smart Scheduling
Built-in booking engine with Google Calendar sync, availability windows, slot management, and automatic email/webhook notifications.

1
Call Arrives
A caller dials in (or your campaign dials out). The telephony provider routes the call to SnapServe and opens a real-time audio stream.
2
Speech is Transcribed
The caller's voice is streamed to your chosen ASR provider. Transcription is returned in under 500ms with support for Indic and multilingual speech.
3
AI Generates a Response
The transcript is sent to the LLM with your agent's persona, system prompt, caller memory, and any business tools. The AI decides what to say — or what action to take (book appointment, transfer, look up data).
4
Voice is Synthesised
The AI response is converted to natural-sounding speech by your TTS provider and streamed back to the caller in real time. Barge-in detection lets callers interrupt naturally.
5
Memory & Analytics Updated
After the call, SnapServe automatically summarises the conversation, updates the caller's memory profile, logs outcomes, and triggers any configured webhooks or CRM updates.
