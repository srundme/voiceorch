## Notes for the founder

- Keep it under ~200 words in the body — investors skim. The version above is intentionally tight.
- Personalise the first line ("I'm reaching out because you've backed [portfolio co] / invest in India SaaS…") — a single specific sentence dramatically lifts reply rates.
- Lead with **what it is**, then the **wedge**, then the **ask** — that order survives a 10-second skim.
- All forward numbers (TAM, 18-month goal) are framed as projections, not traction. Don't add revenue figures you don't have.
- Attach the deck as a **PDF** (export from the deck), not a link, for cold sends.
