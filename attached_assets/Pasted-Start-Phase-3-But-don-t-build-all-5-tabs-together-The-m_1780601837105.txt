Important Rule

For Phase 3:

Do not build CRM.

Do not build Analytics.

Do not build Timeline.

Do not build Queue Engine + 4 queue modules simultaneously.

Build:

Live Queue

until it feels production-ready.

Then move sequentially.

If I were running the project, I would officially define the next sprint as:

Sprint 3A

Campaign Live Queue Workspace

Everything else waits until that is complete. That gives users a real operational surface immediately and creates the foundation for Callbacks, Incoming Calls, Bookings, and Transfers.
