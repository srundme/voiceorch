Looking at the doc, here are the two areas that are likely off:

**1. STT — Silence-based turn triggering is too rigid**

The doc says VAD buffers audio and triggers a turn when silence exceeds `vadSilenceMs` (default 250ms). The problem: with streaming STT (Deepgram/Sarvam), end-of-turn detection should come from `utterance_end_ms` / `vad_events` from the STT provider itself — not a local silence timer. If both are running simultaneously, you get a race condition where the local silence fires first, cuts the audio stream mid-sentence, and sends an incomplete buffer to STT. Result: truncated transcripts.

**2. Barge-in — Energy threshold is too coarse**

The doc says if `rmsEnergy > 1200` for 4+ consecutive frames → interrupt TTS. The problem: RMS energy of the *outgoing TTS audio being played back* can bleed into the incoming audio measurement (especially on telephony where audio is full-duplex over the same socket). The AI ends up barging in on itself. Also, 4 frames at 20ms each = 80ms — that's a very short confirmation window that will cause false positives on plosive sounds or line noise.
