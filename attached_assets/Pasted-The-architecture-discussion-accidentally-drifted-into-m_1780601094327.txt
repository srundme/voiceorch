When Separate Sidebar Modules Make Sense

Only if you want a cross-campaign operations team.

Example:

100 campaigns
50,000 leads

Operations Manager

He wants:

Show ALL callbacks
Show ALL bookings
Show ALL transfers

across every campaign.

Then separate sidebar items make sense.

What I Think You Actually Want

Based on all our previous discussions:

You seem to want:

Campaign
   ↓
Batch
   ↓
Operations

not

Operations
   ↓
Campaign

So I would tell Replit:

Remove Callbacks, Incoming Calls, Bookings, Transfers, Lead CRM, Analytics, and DNC from the global sidebar. Keep only Command Center and Campaigns. Move operational views inside Campaign Detail as tabs. The Command Center can remain a global cross-campaign view, but day-to-day work should happen inside a campaign workspace. The current sidebar makes the platform feel fragmented and exposes too many operational entities at the root level.
