## Slide 3: The Problem

* **Core Problem:** Customer voice operations in India are broken due to high human call-center attrition/costs, while existing voice AI platforms remain stateless and struggle with regional languages.


* **Who Suffers:** High-volume outbound operations in BFSI, lending, insurance, and collections who face dropped contexts, missed follow-ups, and severe US-hosted latency.


* **Why It Matters:** Traditional call setups fail to maintain continuity, and global solutions ignore Indian regional accents, code-switching, and compliance mandates.


* **Key Stat:** "India's BPM and contact-center industry exceeds $40B, where voice remains the dominant channel—yet human agents forget context between calls."



---

## Slide 4: The Solution

* **Product Name:** SnapServe


* **Description:** An autonomous voice AI platform featuring persistent cross-call memory and an instant, zero-code 'Vibe Builder' for non-technical enterprises.


* **Benefit 1 (Persistent Memory):** Caller history, past commitments, and context carry seamlessly across interactions—conversations resume instead of restarting.


* **Benefit 2 (Contextual Auto-Redial):** If a cellular network call drops mid-sentence, the AI agent automatically redials and resumes exactly where it left off.


* **Benefit 3 (Native Localization):** Built-in native handling for Tamil, Hinglish, and Indian accents with low in-country latency and native IRDAI/TRAI/DPDP compliance rules.


* **Current Stage:** Product Ready / Live in Production.



---

## Slide 5: Product/Service – How It Works

* **Step 1 (Describe):** The user types a simple plain-language brief detailing what the agent does (e.g., *"An insurance renewal agent that speaks Tamil and remembers past customer commitments"*).


* **Step 2 (Auto-Configure):** SnapServe automatically maps the best localized STT, LLM, and TTS engines, builds the system prompt, and applies TRAI/DPDP guardrails without developer intervention.


* **Step 3 (Go Live):** The user links an existing corporate telephony line, and the agent begins handling live, memory-mapped production calls in under 5 minutes.

## Slide 9: Key Risks & Mitigation

* **Risk 1 (Platform Competition):** Larger, general-purpose orchestration platforms competing purely on baseline call features.


* *Mitigation:* Our standalone memory proxy layer allows companies already utilizing external orchestrators to plug into SnapServe directly via an `api_base` swap, letting us capture value inside their ecosystems.




* **Risk 2 (Regulatory Tightening):** Sudden, strict shifts in TRAI, IRDAI, or DPDP compliance guidelines impacting data privacy.


* *Mitigation:* Enterprise compliance parameters are engineered directly into our zero-code building blocks from the ground up, making our architecture compliant-by-design.




* **Risk 3 (Dialect Latency):** Degradation of user conversation flows when using multi-layered speech models across regional dialects.


* *Mitigation:* Our infrastructure uses provider-agnostic TypeScript orchestration, letting us dynamically swap in the fastest or most affordable STT/TTS engine at any point.

## Annexures

### Annexure 1: The Tech Stack (Provider-Agnostic Architecture)

* Engineered entirely in a lightweight, enterprise-ready TypeScript framework.


* Decoupled architecture that acts as an intelligent orchestration middleware rather than locking customers into a single model provider.


* Dynamically handles real-time swapping of underlying Speech-to-Text, Large Language Model, and Text-to-Speech engines depending on whichever framework provides the highest accuracy or lowest cost per localized dialect.



### Annexure 2: The Memory Layer (How the Moat Works)

* Operates as an independent context caching layer that intercepts conversational turns.


* Extracts structural facts, transactional tracking variables, and operational commitments from historical user interactions.


* Exposes an OpenAI-compatible interface, enabling external developers to gain deep consumer memory capabilities simply by changing their backend endpoint configuration to point at SnapServe.



### Annexure 3: Enterprise Compliance Matrix

* **TRAI Regulations:** Dynamic checking of Do-Not-Disturb (DND) registries, outbound dialer time limitations, and explicit consent tracking records.


* **DPDP Act Integration:** Strict data localization parameters ensuring in-country storage pipelines, secure localized voice data hashing, and immediate data purging requests.


* **IRDAI Standards:** Built-in template matching for policy verification disclosures, ensuring insurance scripts strictly follow mandatory consumer notification paths.
