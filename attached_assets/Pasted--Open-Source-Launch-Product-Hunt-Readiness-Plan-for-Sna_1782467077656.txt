# Open Source Launch & Product Hunt Readiness Plan for SnapServe AI

We are preparing to launch SnapServe AI as an open-source project and simultaneously launch it on Product Hunt.

I need you to act as both an Open Source Architect and Product Launch Consultant.

Your objective is to create a complete launch strategy and implement everything required before making the GitHub repository public.

## Deliverables

Provide a complete implementation plan covering:

### 1. Open Source Strategy

Help determine the best approach for the first public release.

Questions to answer:

* What parts of SnapServe AI should be open sourced?
* Should we open source the complete application (Frontend + Backend)?
* Should we separate the SaaS platform from the open-source core?
* Which features should remain proprietary?
* What is the recommended architecture for an open-core business model?

Provide your recommendation with pros and cons.
