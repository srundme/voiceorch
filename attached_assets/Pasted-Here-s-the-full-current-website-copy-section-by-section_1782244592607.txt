7. Smart Reconnect (Flagship)
Badge: Flagship

Headline: Network drops. SnapServe calls back.

Body 1: When a call disconnects due to network issues, SnapServe doesn't wait for the caller to dial again. Within a second, it initiates an outbound callback — apologizes for the disconnect, and resumes exactly where the conversation left off.

Body 2: This isn't a system-prompt trick. It's orchestration with memory and intent — the agent knows to call back if they asked, and knows what to say without asking the same details again.

Trait	Description
Detects disconnects
Network drops, signal loss, abrupt hangups — SnapServe knows the difference.
Calls back in < 1 second
Outbound callback fires automatically. No queue. No human handoff.
Opens with context
"Sorry, we got disconnected due to network" — then continues the exact thread.
Never re-asks
Policy numbers, preferences, dispositions — already in memory. Zero repetition.
