Biggest UX Principle

Instead of:

Campaign
 ↓
Callbacks
 ↓
Bookings
 ↓
Transfers

Use:

Campaign
 ↓
Operations
 ↓
Work To Be Done

Users should never think:

"Should I go to Callbacks, Bookings, Transfers, or Incoming Calls?"

Users should think:

"Show me what needs attention."

That's the difference between a feature-rich product and an easy-to-use product.

Final Structure
Campaign

├── Overview
├── Operations
├── Leads
├── Analytics
└── Settings

Everything else (batches, callbacks, incoming calls, bookings, transfers) becomes part of those workflows rather than separate destinations.

That would be the most intuitive, scalable, and enterprise-friendly UX for SnapServe.
