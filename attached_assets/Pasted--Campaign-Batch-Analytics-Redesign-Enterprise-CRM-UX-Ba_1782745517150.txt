# Campaign & Batch Analytics Redesign (Enterprise CRM UX)

## Background

After building the Campaign and Batch modules, we've identified a UX limitation.

The current implementation mixes multiple concepts together, making it difficult for users to understand campaign performance at a glance or filter leads efficiently.

Currently we have:

- Status
- Outcome
- Disposition

However, these fields are overlapping in meaning.

For example:

Status = Completed

Outcome = No Contact

Failure = Busy

These are actually three different dimensions of the same call.

As campaigns scale to thousands or hundreds of thousands of leads, this design becomes difficult to navigate, filter, export, and analyze.

We need to redesign the Campaign and Batch experience to be enterprise-grade.

---

# Core Principle

Separate every call into independent dimensions instead of one mixed status.

Each lead should answer four different questions:

1. What happened to the call?
2. What business outcome did the AI achieve?
3. If it failed, why?
4. What is the next action?

---

# 1. Execution Status

Represents the lifecycle of the call.

Examples:

- Pending
- Queued
- Scheduled
- Calling
- Ringing
- Connected
- In Progress
- Completed
- Interrupted
- Cancelled
- Failed
- Expired
- Skipped

This should be the primary "Status" column.

---

# 2. Business Outcome

Represents the business result after the conversation.

Examples:

- Booked
- Interested
- Callback Requested
- Transferred
- Qualified
- Disqualified
- No Contact
- Wrong Number
- DND
- Already Purchased
- Not Interested
- Spam
- Voicemail

This should replace the current Outcome implementation.

---

# 3. Failure Reason

Failures should never be mixed into Status or Outcome.

Instead create a dedicated Failure Reason.

Examples:

- Busy
- No Answer
- Network Failure
- Telephony Error
- LLM Error
- ASR Error
- TTS Error
- Invalid Number
- Carrier Rejected
- Low Wallet Balance
- Daily Limit Reached
- Outside Business Hours
- Webhook Failure
- Timeout

If the call succeeds this field remains empty.

---

# 4. Retry Status

Retry information deserves its own state.

Examples:

- Retry Scheduled
- Retry Pending
- Retrying
- Max Attempts Reached
- Retry Completed

Attempts column should become richer.

Instead of:

1/2

Display:

Attempt 1 of 2

Hover:

Attempt 1
Busy

Attempt 2
Completed

---

# 5. Callback Status

Separate callback from outcome.

Possible values:

- None
- Requested
- Scheduled
- Completed
- Expired

---

# 6. Lead State

Every lead should have an overall lifecycle.

Examples:

Open

Working

Closed

Archived

Closed

↓

# Why This Change?

The current implementation works for small test campaigns but does not scale for production usage.

By separating Execution Status, Business Outcome, Failure Reason, Retry Status, Callback Status, and Lead State, users can quickly identify operational issues, measure campaign effectiveness, and take action without manually interpreting call records.

This redesign aligns the product with enterprise sales engagement platforms and contact center solutions. It provides clear operational visibility, richer analytics, better filtering, powerful exports, and a scalable data model for future features such as AI insights, SLA monitoring, campaign optimization, and customer journey analytics.

The objective is to make SnapServe AI's Campaign module one of the strongest operational dashboards for AI-powered outbound calling, enabling managers to monitor campaigns in real time while giving agents and administrators actionable insights at both the campaign and batch level.
