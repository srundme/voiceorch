# Campaign Behavior Improvements

## Issue 01: Calling Hours Toggle

Currently, Calling Hours can be configured at the Campaign level. However, once a Calling Hours schedule is saved, there is no way for the user to disable or clear it.

### Expected Behavior

- Add a toggle to enable or disable Calling Hours.
- When the toggle is **ON**, the configured business hours should be enforced.
- When the toggle is **OFF**, Calling Hours should be ignored and the campaign should operate 24/7.
- The previously configured schedule should be preserved in the background, so if the user enables the toggle again, the same schedule is restored without requiring reconfiguration.
- Also provide a **Clear Schedule** option if the user wants to permanently remove the configured business hours.

---

## Issue 02: Website/Form-Based Campaign Lifecycle

Website and Form-based campaigns are **continuous lead sources**, unlike CSV imports.

These campaigns should **never automatically move to the "Completed" status** after processing existing leads.

### Expected Behavior

The campaign should always remain active and continue listening for new leads from its configured source.

Example sources:

- Website Forms
- Meta Lead Ads
- CRM Webhooks
- Public Webhooks
- Landing Page Forms

If no leads are currently available, the campaign should simply remain in an **Idle / Listening** state while waiting for new incoming leads.

---

### Calling Hours Behavior

If a new lead arrives outside configured business hours:

- The lead should **not** be discarded.
- The lead should immediately be added to the campaign queue with a status such as **Queued** or **Waiting for Business Hours**.
- When the next business window begins, queued leads should be processed automatically.
- Queued leads should be prioritized before processing newly arriving leads.
- Throughout this process, the campaign must continue listening for additional incoming leads.

The campaign should never stop accepting new leads simply because it is outside business hours.

## Campaign Control Buttons

Currently, users can manually activate Website/Form-based campaigns.

This behavior should be updated.

### Expected Behavior

After a Website/Form-based campaign is configured successfully:

- The campaign should automatically remain in the **Active / Listening** state.
- Users should not need to manually activate it after each batch of leads.

Users should only have the following control options:

### Pause

- Temporarily stop processing outbound calls.
- Continue accepting and storing incoming leads.
- Resume processing when the user clicks **Resume**.

### Stop

- Stop accepting new leads.
- Stop outbound calling.
- Disconnect the campaign from active processing.
- The campaign status becomes **Stopped**.

Once a campaign is stopped, the primary action button should change to:

**Activate**

Clicking **Activate** should:

- Re-enable webhook/listener processing.
- Resume accepting new incoming leads.
- Continue using the existing campaign configuration.
- No additional setup should be required.

---

## Desired Campaign Lifecycle

Website / Form Campaign

↓

Active (Listening)

↓

New Lead Arrives

↓

Within Business Hours?

├── Yes → Trigger AI Call Immediately
│
└── No → Add Lead to Queue

↓

Next Business Window Opens

↓

Process Queued Leads (Priority)

↓

Continue Listening for New Leads

This ensures Website, Meta Ads, CRM, and Webhook campaigns behave like always-on lead ingestion pipelines rather than one-time import campaigns.
