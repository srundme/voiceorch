Slide 3: The Problem
Core Problem: Customer voice operations in India are broken. For massive enterprises, human call centers are high-cost and suffer from high attrition. For millions of growing SMBs, missing a call means losing a paying customer because they lack the technical teams to build automated solutions.

Who suffers from it: High-volume operations in BFSI/lending, alongside mid-market e-commerce brands, regional retailers, and local services where every dropped call is lost revenue.

Why it matters:

For Enterprises: Dropped contexts, missed follow-ups, and severe US-hosted latency.

For SMBs: A complete barrier to automation. They are forced to rely on chaotic manual follow-ups because current AI voice tools require developers to deploy.

KEY STAT: "India's BPM industry exceeds $40B (Source: NASSCOM), with voice as the largest channel—yet human agents forget context between calls."

Slide 4: The Solution
Product/Service Name: SnapServe

What it does: SnapServe is an autonomous voice AI platform featuring persistent cross-call memory and an instant, zero-code 'Vibe Builder' for non-technical users.

Benefit 1 (Persistent Memory): Caller history, past commitments, and context carry seamlessly across interactions—conversations resume, not restart.

Benefit 2 (Contextual Auto-Redial): If a cellular network call drops mid-sentence, the AI agent automatically redials and resumes exactly where it left off.

Benefit 3 (Native Localization): Built-in native handling for Tamil, Hinglish, and regional accents with low in-country latency and native IRDAI/TRAI/DPDP compliance rules.

CURRENT STAGE: Product Ready / Live in production.

Slide 5: Product/Service - How It Works
Step 1 (Describe): The user types a simple plain-language brief detailing what the agent does (e.g., "An insurance renewal agent that speaks Tamil and remembers past customer commitments").

Step 2 (Auto-Configure): SnapServe automatically maps the best localized STT, LLM, and TTS engines, builds the system prompt, and applies TRAI/DPDP guardrails without developer intervention.

Step 3 (Go Live): The user links a corporate telephony line, and the agent begins handling live, memory-mapped production calls in under 5 minutes.
