Yes. I would approve that architecture change immediately.

Moving lead_sources from campaign-scoped to workspace-scoped is the correct enterprise decision.

Benefits:

Configure a Meta Form once and reuse forever.
Configure a Webhook once and reuse forever.
Source analytics become cleaner.
Future integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Google Forms, APIs) become reusable assets.
Campaigns become consumers of sources instead of owners of sources.

The critical addition:

Workspace
   └── Lead Sources
          ├── Meta Form A
          ├── Meta Form B
          ├── Website Form
          ├── Webhook XYZ
          └── CSV Source

Then:

Campaign A
   └── Batch 1
          └── Source: Meta Form A

Campaign B
   └── Batch 2
          └── Source: Meta Form A
