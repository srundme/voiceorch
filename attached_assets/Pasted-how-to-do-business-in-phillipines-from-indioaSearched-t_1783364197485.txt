how to do business in phillipines from indioaSearched the webSearched the webHere's the full practical breakdown for an Indian founder wanting to do business in the Philippines:

The honest reality first
For a SaaS/AI product like VoiceCRM, you don't need to physically set up in Philippines initially. You can sell to Philippine BPOs from India and only formalize once you have paying customers. That's the smart path.

Three routes, in order of effort:
Route 1 — Sell from India (start here)
Zero registration needed. You sell VoiceCRM as a SaaS to Philippine BPO clients, invoice in USD, receive via Wise or Stripe. Completely legal. This is how most Indian SaaS companies enter foreign markets. Only formalize once you hit meaningful revenue.
Route 2 — Representative Office (when you need local presence)
A representative office is limited to non-commercial activities — marketing, liaison, client support. It cannot earn revenue locally. Minimum capital requirement is just $30,000. Good for having a local contact person, attending meetings, building relationships without full legal commitment.
Route 3 — Full Company Registration (when you're serious)
For IT and SaaS businesses, 100% foreign ownership is permitted. The minimum paid-up capital for full foreign ownership is $200,000. Registration typically takes 20–35 business days.
You must appoint at least one resident director (can be a foreigner or Filipino), a Filipino corporate secretary, and a resident treasurer.

The 5 registration steps if you go full entity:
Register with SEC → Get Barangay Clearance → Get Mayor's Office business permit → Register with BIR (Bureau of Internal Revenue) → Register with SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG as employer.
Taxes: Corporate income tax 25%, VAT 12%, plus social security contributions.

Smartest path for VoiceCRM specifically:
Phase 1 (now) — Sell from India. Find 1-2 Philippine BPO clients, sign contracts in USD, deliver the product remotely. No registration needed.
Phase 2 (₹50L+ revenue from PH) — Set up a Representative Office in Manila ($30K capital). Hire one local business development person who attends meetings and demos on your behalf.
Phase 3 (serious scale) — Full domestic corporation with a Filipino co-founder or local director. Register with BOI (Board of Investments) for tax incentives under the CREATE Act.

India-Philippines specific advantage:
India and Philippines have a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) — so you won't be taxed twice on the same income in both countries. This makes the India-first model even cleaner financially.
Practical first step: Sign up on Wise Business to receive USD payments from Philippine clients without losing money on forex. That alone unblocks you to start selling immediately without any registration.
