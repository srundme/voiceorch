# Brag Plan: SnapServe Meeting Agents

## What is this app?
SnapServe is a voice-agent platform. Meeting bots send that same agent into Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams — on camera, speaking, and driving the slides.

## The angle
**The empty chair just got filled.** Not a morph gimmick. A dark studio. Three rooms. One agent sits down, talks, then presents the deck.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A huge faded three-bar sculpture turns in the dark. The official mark locks in. Line: **Your agent just sat down.**

## Key moments
- Three room cards (Meet / Zoom / Teams) rise; the agent sits on Meet
- Real Google Meet plate: agent live, caption speaking
- Real presenting plate: deck reveals, `next_slide` flips 3/12 → 4/12

## Outro / punchline
Three-bar mark. **Meeting agents. Live now.** app.snapserve.ai

## User flow worth showing
Paste a Meet link → agent is in the room → agent presents and advances the slide.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium dark studio, voiceover-ready
- Interpretation: slow confidence, one idea per beat, music stays under VO

## Format: square — 1080x1080
## Duration: 24 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#050505` studio with a giant three-bar sculpture (opacities 1 / .60 / .36)
- Accent: grey `#A3A3A3` / white
- Text: `#FFFFFF` / `#B8B8B8`
- Display font: Tex Gyre Heros (wordmark) + Inter
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual: official three-bar mark + headset agent + meeting plates

## Share copy (draft)
Your agent just sat down in the meeting. It talks. It presents the deck.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse cinematic support under a future voiceover
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12` at **0.18** (50% of the last bed)
- Music treatment: fade in 0.4s, hold under VO, fade last 0.6s
- Music cue guidance: vol-12 preset — lock rooms ~8.74, presenting ~17.47, end ~22.93
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (VO will own the mix)
- SFX posture: sparse, quiet (0.28–0.40)
- Restraint rule: never compete with voiceover; no dense SFX beds
