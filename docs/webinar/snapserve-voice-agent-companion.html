What is a voice agent?

A voice agent is software that can take a real phone call. It listens, understands, speaks back, and can finish a small job while it talks.

Not this

A robot menu

“Press 1 for sales. Press 2 for support.” The caller does all the work.

Not this

Only a chat box

A website bot that types. Useful, but it is not a phone conversation.

This

A trained teammate on the phone

The caller talks in normal language. The agent answers, asks one question at a time, and follows the rules you wrote. It never sleeps, and it can take many calls at once.

A simple picture

A parent calls a clinic: “Do you have a slot tomorrow evening?”

1. Hear

The agent catches the words, even if the line is a little noisy.

2. Decide

It checks your hours and what you allow it to book.

3. Act

It books or offers a time, then can send WhatsApp after the call.

Good first jobs

  • Book or confirm an appointment
  • Answer fees, hours, and location
  • Follow up a lead: interested or not
  • Collect name, city, and a callback time

Leave these to a person

  • Angry or upset callers
  • Odd complaints you have not written down
  • Promises you have not approved
  • Anything that needs a human yes

The only diagram you need

How a phone call actually works

Five parts. Remember these and the rest of the hour will click.

1

Number

They dial you, or SnapServe dials them. It is a real phone number.

2 Listen

Speech to text

Their voice becomes words. People call this STT.

3 Think

The brain

Your instructions plus their words. Decides the next sentence. People call this the LLM.

4 Speak

Text to speech

Those words become a natural voice. People call this TTS.

5 Do

Actions

WhatsApp, a sheet, a CRM, or a label when the call ends.

The caller only hears a conversation

You design the job behind it. If the reply feels slow, that wait is called latency. Shorter feels more human. If the voice sounds off, that is usually the speaking step, not the thinking step.

Business logic, in plain English

These are the rules the agent must not break. Write them before you worry about the voice.

Never promise a price or discount you did not write down.

Ask one question at a time. Confirm name, city, or time before you close.

If they say they are not interested, thank them and end politely. Do not push.

Send outcome WhatsApp after the call, when you know Interested vs Not interested. During the call you are still guessing.

Do this on paper first

Design the conversation

The live build goes faster if you already know the job. One sentence is enough to start.

Write this now

This agent [does what] for [whom] so that [result].

This agent follows up loan leads for first-time callers so interested people get a callback today.

Four beats of almost every call

1

Hello

Who you are. Why you called, or how you can help.

2

Listen

One question at a time. Do not dump a list.

3

Confirm

Repeat the facts that matter: name, time, city.

4

Close

What happens next. Then a short goodbye.

Too much

“Hi I am an advanced AI solution from our omnichannel stack here to digitally transform your enquiry journey.”

Better

“Hi, I am Aarthi from Green Valley Clinic. I can help you book a visit. Is now a good time?”

The live 30 minutes

Build it on SnapServe

We will do this together. You can repeat the same six steps after the session at app.snapserve.ai.

1

Create the agent

Give it a name, a language, and a voice. This is the teammate, not the whole company.

2

Write the conversation

Paste the one-sentence job and the four beats. Say what success sounds like.

3

Add business logic

What to collect. What never to say. When to end. When to mark Interested or Not interested.

4

Test in the browser

Talk to it before it touches a phone. Fix the greeting and the close here. It is free to be messy.

5

Put it on a number

Inbound: they call you. Outbound: you call them. Same agent. Different direction.

6

Improve from one real call

Listen once. Change one instruction. Test again. That loop is the whole craft.

After the call

Outcome messages

When the call ends you know the result. Map Interested to one WhatsApp template, Not interested to another. Leave mid-call send off unless the person asks for something during the call.

PromptThe written job for the agent. Your one sentence plus rules.
STT / TTSListening (speech to text) and speaking (text to speech).

LatencyHow long until the agent replies. Shorter feels more human.
DispositionThe label at the end: Interested, Not interested, Callback, Wrong number.
CampaignCalling many people from a list, with retry rules.
