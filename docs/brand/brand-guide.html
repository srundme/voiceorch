Do

  • Use the three-bar mark + SnapServe wordmark from this kit
  • Keep product UI ink / white
  • Reserve saffron / teal / indigo for marketing
  • Keep SnapServe first when co-branding with Sarvam
  • Clear space ≥ ½ mark height; min mark 16×16 px (prefer 20–24)

Don’t

  • Use the green logo.svg placeholder
  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark
  • Recolor bars arbitrarily
  • Write “Snap Serve” as two words
  • Stack competing logos above SnapServe
