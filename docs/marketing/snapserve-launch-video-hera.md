# SnapServe launch video — Hera script

Paste-ready storyboard for [Hera](https://hera.video) using the SnapServe Brand Kit.
Target length **40 seconds**, 9 sections. Facts here are true of the shipped product —
do not add user counts, revenue, or a launch date to a public cut.

Product: [app.snapserve.ai](https://app.snapserve.ai)

---

## 1. Brand Kit settings

| Field | Value | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Company name | `SnapServe` | One word, capital S twice |
| Logo | `docs/brand/assets/snapserve-mark.svg` | Ink mark, correct on a light background. Never the green `logo.svg` |
| Font | Inter 600 | Matches app UI sans |
| Background | `#FAFAFA` | Keep |
| Text | `#121213` | Keep (close to brand ink `#0F172A`) |
| **Accent** | **`#14B8A6`** | Change from `#C2C2C6` — grey accent reads as disabled, nothing pops |
| **Rhythm** | **Snappy and punchy** | A 40s launch cut needs cuts, not drift |

Brand gradient for the end card only:
`linear-gradient(135deg, #FF9933 0%, #14B8A6 50%, #6366F1 100%)`

### Guidelines box (paste verbatim)

```
Write "SnapServe" as one word, capital S on Snap and capital S on Serve.
Indian business context: rupees, +91 numbers, Indian names like Priya and Ramesh.
Keep frames calm and light. Rounded corners. Generous whitespace.
Accent teal for emphasis only, never full-bleed colour blocks.
No stock photos of Western call centres. No headset-and-cubicle imagery.
Never claim customer counts, revenue, awards, or "India's #1".
```

## 2. Storyboard

Each section: **on-screen text** (short — Hera animates it) + **voiceover** + **visual**.

### 1 — Hook
- **On-screen:** `The lead called at 9 PM.`
- **VO:** "A customer enquires at 9 PM."
- **Visual:** Dark phone mockup, single missed-call notification.

### 2 — Problem
- **On-screen:** `Nobody called back.`
- **VO:** "By morning, they have already bought from someone else."
- **Visual:** Same screen, notification fades to grey.

### 3 — Product reveal
- **On-screen:** `SnapServe`
- **VO:** "SnapServe answers, in the language they speak."
- **Visual:** Logo on `#FAFAFA`, teal underline sweep.

### 4 — Languages
- **On-screen:** `Tamil · Hindi · Telugu · English`
- **VO:** "Indic-first voice agents on a real plus-nine-one number."
- **Visual:** Waveform, language labels cycling.

### 5 — Memory (the differentiator)
- **On-screen:** `It remembers Priya.`
- **VO:** "It remembers who called last time, so nobody repeats themselves."
- **Visual:** Caller memory card — name, last call, notes.

### 6 — Campaigns
- **On-screen:** `Upload leads. It dials.`
- **VO:** "Upload a sheet and it dials, retries the no-answers, and books the visit."
- **Visual:** Screen recording of Campaigns → batch running.

### 7 — After the call
- **On-screen:** `WhatsApp sent automatically.`
- **VO:** "The moment a call ends, the follow-up goes out on WhatsApp."
- **Visual:** WhatsApp message sliding in beside a call summary.

### 8 — Proof / pricing
- **On-screen:** `₹5 per minute. Prepaid.`
- **VO:** "Five rupees a minute. No seats, no lock-in."
- **Visual:** Wallet balance + transcript list in History.

### 9 — End card
- **On-screen:** `app.snapserve.ai`
- **VO:** "SnapServe. Your agents, on the phone, today."
- **Visual:** Brand gradient band under the wordmark.

## 3. Screen recordings to capture first

Record at 1440×900, light mode, demo workspace only (never a real customer's data):

1. `/app/agents` — agent builder with a named agent
2. `/app/campaigns` — a batch mid-dial with live status
3. `/app/history` — call list, then one transcript open
4. `/app/memory` — a caller memory card
5. A phone screen showing the after-call WhatsApp message

Blur or replace: real phone numbers, customer names, wallet amounts.
