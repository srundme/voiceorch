## What SnapServe is

SnapServe is software that runs an **AI voice agent on the phone** - like a well-trained telecaller who can work all day, in the caller's language.

It can:

- Call your customers, or answer when they call you
- Speak naturally - not a robot menu of "press 1"
- Book appointments, answer common questions, collect details, and follow up on payments
- Send a **WhatsApp** or email after the call
- Write the result into a spreadsheet or your CRM

You do not hire a call-centre floor to start. You set up one agent, give it your script and your data, and it dials.

## How a call works (no jargon)

The person talks. SnapServe turns speech into text. The AI decides what to say or do (book a slot, send WhatsApp, transfer to a human, end the call). It speaks back on the same phone line. You can listen to recordings and see what happened after each call.

## How we would implement it with you

1. **Pick one country and one job.** Example: Kenya - English + Swahili, callback after a website form. Or Nigeria - English + Hausa/Yoruba, appointment reminder.
2. **Get a local number** and confirm calling rules (consent, DND, POPIA in South Africa, NDPR in Nigeria, and similar).
3. **Write the agent once:** who you are, what you sell, what you must never promise, when to transfer to a human.
4. **Connect your list:** CSV, Google Sheet, or website form. SnapServe dials.
5. **Pilot 100-500 calls.** Listen to recordings, fix the script, then scale.
6. **Add WhatsApp** so the customer gets a message after the call (booking, link, summary).

A first live pilot is usually days, not months, if the use case is clear.

## What we would not claim

SnapServe was built first for India (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and other Indian languages on the phone). That is why the voice quality there is very strong. For Africa we use the global voice stack, and we should prove Swahili / Hausa / Yoruba on **your** numbers before a big campaign. English, French, and Arabic we can start with more confidence.
