# Pitch Deck Checklist & Mistakes

## Essential slides (general / seed+)

1. **Company overview** — name, tagline, one-sentence what you do, mission  
2. **Problem** — pain, scope, why current solutions fail  
3. **Solution** — product, differentiation, why compelling  
4. **Market** — TAM / serviceable market, growth dynamics (with proof)  
5. **Product / tech** — demo/screens, IP, roadmap highlights  
6. **Business model** — revenue, unit economics, pricing, LTV/CAC  
7. **Go-to-market** — acquisition, sales/marketing, channels, growth levers  
8. **Traction** — growth metrics, testimonials, pilots, revenue  
9. **Team** — founders, key hires, advisors/investors, why you win  
10. **Ask** — amount, use of funds, milestones, ideal investor  

For **pre-seed**, also prioritize **Why now**, **Competition**, **Validation**, **Roadmap** — see `pre-seed-deck.md`.

## Checklist (7 Cs)

- **Concise:** 15–20 slides max (shorter pre-seed)  
- **Clear:** simple language, minimal jargon  
- **Compelling:** strong narrative arc  
- **Credible:** data-backed, realistic projections  
- **Complete:** answers key investor questions for the stage  
- **Customised:** tailored to the investor when possible  
- **Current:** updated metrics  
- **Consistent:** visual + verbal alignment  
- **Confident:** belief without arrogance  

## Content mistakes

- Product obsession, weak business story  
- Top-down TAM fantasy without bottom-up  
- Jargon / complexity  
- Hiding metrics  
- Weak team slide  
- Unrealistic financials  

## Design mistakes

- Too much text (aim ~30 words max / slide for live pitch)  
- Poor hierarchy / tiny fonts  
- Inconsistent branding  
- Charts without a one-line takeaway  
- No visuals  

## Delivery mistakes

- Reading slides  
- Can’t handle questions / gets defensive  
- Talking too much, listening too little  
- No passion or conviction  
- Unprepared for Zoom seated dynamics  

## Quality bar before sending a deck

- [ ] Stage-appropriate length and emphasis  
- [ ] Problem → solution → why now linked tightly  
- [ ] One clear ask and use of funds  
- [ ] Traction or validation appropriate to stage  
- [ ] Team slide shows why *this* team  
- [ ] Numbers you can defend cold  
- [ ] Skimmable in ~3 minutes  
- [ ] Leave-behind still makes sense without the speaker (or you attach speaker notes)
