# SnapServe — Knowledge Base

## What is SnapServe?
SnapServe (app.snapserve.ai) is an Indic-first AI voice agent platform. Businesses use it to create AI agents that talk to customers on phone calls, browser calls, WhatsApp, and Google Meet — in Indian languages like Tamil, Hindi, and English, including natural code-mixed speech (Tanglish/Hinglish).

## Who is it for?
Any business that talks to customers on the phone: clinics booking appointments, real-estate teams qualifying leads, schools and coaching centres, e-commerce support, loan and insurance follow-ups, restaurants taking orders, and event registrations. No coding needed — everything is set up from the dashboard.

## Core features

### AI Voice Agents
- Create an agent in minutes: write what it should do (its prompt), pick a voice, pick languages.
- Agents understand and speak Indian languages and switch language automatically if the caller switches mid-call.
- Agents can remember returning callers across calls (caller memory) and greet them accordingly.
- Advanced controls: barge-in (caller can interrupt), silence handling, call time limits, voicemail detection, and more.

### Phone calls (inbound & outbound)
- Buy an Indian phone number inside SnapServe and assign it to an agent — it answers inbound calls immediately.
- Place single outbound calls or run bulk campaigns.
- Every call gets a recording, full transcript, and an automatic summary.

### Campaigns
- Upload a CSV of leads and the agent calls them automatically in batches.
- Calling-hours windows, automatic retries for unanswered calls, and rolling concurrency (multiple calls in parallel).
- Post-call dispositions (labels like "Interested", "Call back later") set automatically by the AI.

### Website lead capture
- Connect your website's contact form to SnapServe with a webhook link — the moment someone fills the form, the agent calls them back within seconds.

### Google Sheets automatic calls
- Link a Google Sheet; when a new row is added, the agent calls that person automatically.

### WhatsApp
- Agents can send WhatsApp messages during or after a call — brochures, payment links, booking confirmations.
- Options: instant setup (shared number), your own WhatsApp Business number, or bring your own provider.
- Inbound WhatsApp chatbot replies are also supported.

### Google Meet / video meetings
- A SnapServe agent can join a Google Meet as a participant, listen, and speak — useful for demos, interviews, and support sessions.
- In meetings the agent knows who the participants are and who is speaking, and can address people by name.

### Knowledge Base
- Upload documents (PDF, Word, text, markdown) or FAQs, attach them to an agent, and it answers from your content instead of guessing.

### Squads (multi-agent)
- Multiple agents work together with hand-offs — for example a reception agent transfers to a sales specialist agent mid-call.

### Journeys
- Multi-step lead sequences: call, then WhatsApp follow-up, then a retry call — configured as a flow.

### Human transfer
- The agent can transfer a live call to a real person's phone number whenever the caller asks or the situation needs it.

### Appointment booking
- Agents connect to Google Calendar: they check free slots, book appointments during the call, and send confirmations.

### Webcall links
- Share a browser link so anyone can talk to your agent from a webpage — no phone number needed. Great for testing and website "Talk to us" buttons.

### Analytics & call logs
- Dashboard with call volumes, durations, success rates, dispositions, and per-call drill-down: recording, transcript, summary, and technical logs.

## Languages
- Strong support for Tamil, Hindi, and Indian English, plus other Indic languages depending on the chosen speech providers.
- Agents can be multilingual: they detect the caller's language and reply in the same language, switching mid-call naturally.

## Technology (plain-language)
- SnapServe combines three AI pieces per agent: speech recognition (understands the caller), a language model (decides what to say), and text-to-speech (the voice). You can choose from providers like Sarvam (Indic specialist), Google Gemini, and Cartesia voices — or keep the recommended defaults.
- Telephony is built in; you can also bring your own carrier.

## Pricing & wallet
- SnapServe works on a prepaid wallet: you top up and calls are charged per minute based on your chosen setup.
- Exact per-minute rates are shown in the dashboard's wallet/billing section. For current pricing, check the dashboard or contact the SnapServe team.

## Getting started
1. Sign up at app.snapserve.ai.
2. Create an agent — describe the job, pick a voice and languages.
3. Test it instantly with a browser webcall link.
4. Buy/assign a phone number or upload a lead list to start calling.

## Common questions

**Q: Do I need coding knowledge?**
No. Everything — agents, campaigns, WhatsApp, knowledge bases — is configured from the dashboard. Developers can additionally use the REST API and API keys.

**Q: Can the agent speak Tamil and English mixed, like people actually talk in Chennai?**
Yes. Code-mixed speech is a core strength — the agent both understands and speaks naturally mixed language.

**Q: What happens if the AI can't handle something?**
It can transfer the call to a human number you configure, and everything is logged so you can review it.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
Call recordings, transcripts, and customer data stay in your account. Compliance features like data-retention controls and PII handling are built in.

**Q: Can it make hundreds of calls a day?**
Yes — campaigns handle bulk calling with parallel calls, retries, and calling-hour rules.
